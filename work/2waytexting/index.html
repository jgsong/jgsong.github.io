Contactless 2-Way Texting for a COVID-19 World

My client was a tax prep firm with 1.5 million+ customers across 2,500+ locations. During tax season, franchisees would drive customers to visit their local brick & mortar location to complete a sale. However, when the COVID-19 pandemic started, their business as usual wasn't going to work safely anymore so they began investing in enhancing their in-house marketing texting platform, FusionText, and add 2-Way Texting.

Franchisees could use FusionText to send scheduled or automated messages, and 2-Way Texting would allow them to see replies and have full conversations with customers.

I led design on 2-Way Texting, where over 2 months, I conducted research, created and prioritized a backlog of features for 2-way texting, and designed the feature set for Phase 1. This enabled franchisees to create deeper customer relationships with the over 600k recipients opted-in to receive messages.

My team included a Technical Architect and I collaborated closely with a working group of Marketing, Engineering, and Franchisee stakeholders to prioritize features and gather design feedback.

Learning from Franchisees With 2-Way Texting

The working group provided business, user and technical insights and they shared that some franchisees had already implemented 2-way texting on their own. I interviewed a few of these pioneers to understand potential issues to avoid and successes we could replicate with our design.

  • How might we set expectations on availability with customers?

    Customers expected faster replies compared to calls and emails, which put more strain on employees, especially for locations with fewer staff. Employees stretched too thin could perform worse in their other responsibilities.

    In their personal lives, customers might expect their friends and family to respond at all hours so franchisees needed to communicate availability with customers and handle after-hours messages clearly.

  • How might we help franchisees sound like a real human being and less like a corporate entity?

    Texting was a great way for franchisees to connect with younger customers and pioneering 2-Way Texting franchisees found that having non-verbals - emojis, images, gifs etc helped build rapport and convert customers.

  • How might we minimize potential for abuse?

    Texting could be used to harass employees. Franchisees would sometimes receive inappropriate messages or have upset customers.
