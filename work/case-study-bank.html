The Goal - 01

How might we empower and encourage more customers to install their DIY security system on their own before their activation call?

In 2019 Texas Capital Bank, a regional bank with over $40B in managed assets, launched Bask Bank, a new “digital attacker” online-only bank that rewarded American Airlines points instead of interest. The launch generated significant buzz and interest, driving people to explore the website, but Bask was losing a huge percentage of prospects as they dove into onboarding and began signing up.

My Role

I led and created an end-to-end onboarding redesign prototype to define the north star design. I partnered with the product manager, marketing, and customer support team to uncover the root of why drop-offs were happening and drive design solutions.

Outcomes

After the first set of recommendations hit production, leadership reported a reduction in drop-off rates and a bonus reduction in customer service calls resulting from the changes. We continued to add enhancements until the vendor platform could no longer be customized. The client used the designs to begin an RFP process to find a vendor that could implement the north star design.

How might we understand why prospects drop off during onboarding?

I wasn’t able to speak to prospective customers directly, however I led a data-driven research approach by reviewing user behavior analytics, interviews with customer support agents, competitor analysis, and an end-to-end audit of the existing experience.

Lack of Credibility and Recognition

Asking for Deposits Too Early

Users were being asked the amount they wanted to commit to their initial deposit before their funding account was setup. Asking for a commitment to deposit before Bask is able to make the deposit. How could Bask make the initial funding transfer when it wasn’t even possible?

Unclear Value Proposition

Bask offered attractive sign-up bonuses for initial deposits that met key thresholds, but the step that asked users to set up an initial deposit only had a single deposit input.
