Financial Services Bank

Reducing Onboarding Drop-Offs

PLACEHOLDER

Texas Capital Bank, a regional bank with over $40B in managed assets, launched Bask Bank, a new “digital attacker” bank that provided rewards points instead of interest. While the launch generated significant buzz and interest driving people to explore the website, Bask was losing a large percentage of prospects as they began onboarding and began signing up.

I led a data-driven research approach by reviewing user behavior analytics, interviews with customer support agents, competitor analysis, and an end-to-end audit of the existing experience. I designed a future-state onboarding experience based on the research I conducted.

Outcomes

After the first set of recommendations hit prod, leadership reported a reduction in drop-off rates and a bonus reduction in customer service calls resulting from the changes. We continued to add enhancements until the vendor platform could no longer be customized. The client used the designs to begin an RFP process to find a vendor that could implement the north star design.

Drop-Off Drivers

I'd been hearing about dropoffs from leadership during sprint review. I had already been working on setting up Amplitude, a user behavior analytics tool, for the organization so I volunteered to find out why and come up with solutions with the product owner.
